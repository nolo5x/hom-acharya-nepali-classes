About Hom Acharya

Dedicated to connecting people through Nepali language, education, and culture.

MY STORY

Namaste! I'm Hom Acharya.

I am a professional Nepali language educator with over 27 years of teaching experience. Since 1997, I have taught Nepali to both native and non-native speakers at all proficiency levels.

My teaching experience spans Nepal, Japan, and the United States, where I have worked with individuals and groups from diverse educational and cultural backgrounds.

Throughout my career, I have collaborated with academic institutions, including Tribhuvan University in Nepal and Shimane University in Japan.

Currently, I teach Nepali language as a full-time faculty member at Cornell University in Ithaca, New York.
