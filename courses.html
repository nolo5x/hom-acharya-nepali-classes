Beginner & Elementary Courses

Learn Nepali script, pronunciation, essential vocabulary, and everyday communication skills.

Beginner 1

Nepali Vowels & Consonants

Develop foundational reading and writing skills using Nepali vowel and consonant sounds.

What You'll Learn

  • Recognize vowel and consonant sounds in their standard order.
  • Identify vowels and consonants in random order.
  • Practice words and sentences using combinations of vowel and consonant sounds.

Beginner 2

Maatraa & Nepali Reading

Build on your understanding of Nepali letters by practicing vowel symbols and half letters.

What You'll Learn

  • Attach vowel symbols (Maatraa) to consonants.
  • Practice BahraKhari.
  • Read words and short sentences using different vowel symbols.
  • Learn half letters and practice words and sentences containing them.
