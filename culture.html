LANGUAGE & IDENTITY

More Than Words

Language is an essential social tool that connects individuals, communities, and cultures.

It helps us communicate ideas, build relationships, express identity, and understand the experiences of others.

In my teaching, I believe meaningful Nepali language learning must be connected with cultural understanding.

Students explore the language through authentic cultural experiences and real-world contexts rather than relying only on memorization and grammar exercises.
