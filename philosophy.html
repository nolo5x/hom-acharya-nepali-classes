MY APPROACH TO TEACHING

Teaching Philosophy

Meaningful language learning goes beyond memorizing vocabulary and grammar. It is about communication, cultural understanding, and real-world application.

LEARNING WITH PURPOSE

Learning Nepali Through Experience

I am deeply passionate about teaching the Nepali language and consider it a rewarding and integral part of my academic career.

Every student brings a unique background, learning style, and set of goals. My responsibility is to understand those differences and create an engaging learning experience that responds to individual needs.

My classes emphasize meaningful communication, active participation, and practical language use. Students are encouraged to apply what they learn in authentic everyday situations.

To make instruction more accessible, I also offer interactive online learning through Zoom.

नमस्ते

Learning by Doing

Language develops through meaningful interaction, consistent practice, and personal experience.

MY CORE PRINCIPLES

Six Principles That Guide My Teaching

Each lesson is designed to help students learn confidently, participate actively, and make meaningful progress.

01

Knowing My Students

Understanding students' backgrounds, interests, motivations, and learning goals allows me to personalize instruction and provide meaningful support.

02

Learning Through Application

Learning by doing is central to my classroom. Interactive activities and real-life communication help students actively apply new skills.

03

Cultivating Lifelong Learning

My goal is to inspire lasting interest in Nepali language learning and help students continue practicing independently, even after their courses end.

04

Individualized Support

Students learn at different paces and in different ways. I monitor their progress and adjust instruction to provide the support they need.

05

Facilitator of Learning

I guide and support students while encouraging them to take an active role in learning, set ambitious goals, and challenge themselves.

06

Communicative & Inclusive Learning

My classroom is a supportive environment where students can participate, ask questions, express themselves, and learn from mistakes.

COMPLETE LANGUAGE DEVELOPMENT

Building All Four Language Skills

Nepali language instruction integrates listening, speaking, reading, and writing to develop balanced communication skills.

01

Listening

Develop comprehension through spoken Nepali, dialogues, audio materials, and authentic communication.

02

Speaking

Build confidence through conversation, questions, role-plays, and practical speaking activities.

03

Reading

Progress from Nepali script and short sentences to stories, articles, and literary texts.

04

Writing

Strengthen written expression through vocabulary practice, sentences, assignments, and comprehension responses.
